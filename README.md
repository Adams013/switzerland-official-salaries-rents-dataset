# Switzerland Official Salaries, Rents, Health Insurance & Cost of Living Datasets (2024–2027)

Cleaned, machine-readable CSV and JSON datasets compiled from Swiss federal statistical releases by **[LivingEase](https://livingease.ch)**.

## Included Datasets

1. **`swiss-wages-lse-2024.csv` / `.json`** — Gross monthly and annual wages (P10, P25, Median, P75, P90) across 36 ISCO-08 occupation groups, 7 Swiss statistical regions, and 3 age bands from the Swiss Federal Statistical Office (BFS) Wage Structure Survey (*Lohnstrukturerhebung LSE 2024*).
   - **Methodology & Full Guide (EN):** [Swiss Salary Guide 2026: Official Pay for 36 Occupations](https://livingease.ch/blog/salary-switzerland-by-role-2026)
   - **Methodology & Full Guide (DE):** [Lohn nach Beruf Schweiz 2026: amtliche Löhne für 36 Berufe](https://livingease.ch/blog/de/gehalt-schweiz-nach-beruf-2026)
   - **Regional Breakdown:** [Salary by Canton in Switzerland](https://livingease.ch/blog/salary-by-canton-switzerland)
   - **Interactive Gross-to-Net Calculator:** [Swiss Salary Calculator](https://livingease.ch/salary-calculator)

2. **`swiss-health-premiums-2027.csv` / `.json`** — Official 2027 mandatory basic health insurance (KVG/LAMal) monthly adult premiums across all 26 Swiss cantons (cheapest, median, highest for CHF 300 deductible, and lowest floor premium for CHF 2,500 deductible) from the Federal Office of Public Health (BAG).
   - **Full Analysis:** [Swiss Health Insurance Premiums 2027: Official Figures by Canton](https://livingease.ch/blog/swiss-health-insurance-premiums-2027-official-figures)
   - **Switching Rules (EN):** [How to Cancel Health Insurance in Switzerland](https://livingease.ch/blog/cancel-health-insurance-switzerland)
   - **Switching Rules (DE):** [Krankenkasse kündigen Schweiz: Fristen & Vorlage](https://livingease.ch/blog/de/krankenkasse-kuendigen)

3. **`swiss-cost-of-living-2026.csv` / `.json`** — Single-person monthly budgets across 8 Swiss cities (Zurich, Geneva, Zug, Lausanne, Basel, Bern, Lucerne, Lugano) combining advertised 2-room rents, BAG 2027 cantonal health insurance medians, BFS Household Budget Survey (HABE 2023) everyday spending, and ESTV net take-home pay shares.
   - **Full Guide (EN):** [Cost of Living in Switzerland 2026: Monthly Budget by City](https://livingease.ch/blog/cost-of-living-switzerland-expat)
   - **Full Guide (DE):** [Lebenshaltungskosten Schweiz 2026: Monatsbudget nach Stadt](https://livingease.ch/blog/de/lebenshaltungskosten-schweiz)

4. **`swiss-rents-2026.csv` / `.json`** — Advertised monthly apartment rents by room count (1 to 4+ rooms) vs. BFS Strukturerhebung existing lease rents across 16 Swiss cities.
   - **Full Guide (EN):** [Rent in Switzerland by City](https://livingease.ch/blog/rent-by-city-switzerland)
   - **Full Guide (DE):** [Mietpreise Schweiz nach Stadt](https://livingease.ch/blog/de/mietpreise-schweiz-nach-stadt)
   - **Interactive Tool:** [Swiss Rent Calculator](https://livingease.ch/rent-calculator)
